## Deskripsi lengkap (1718/4000)
```
Tap at the right moment, stack floor after floor, and complete iconic towers from around the world!

Menara Lapis is a relaxing yet addictive one-finger stacking game. Floors slide in from alternating sides. Tap to drop them — whatever overhangs is sliced off, so your tower gets narrower. Land a perfect drop and the floor stays whole; three perfects in a row make it grow wider again!

🏙️ 60 TOWERS, 6 REGIONS
Build towers inspired by famous landmarks: a monument crowned with a golden flame, a clock tower with a horned roof, pagodas, a leaning bell tower, gothic cathedrals, mosaic minarets, art deco skyscrapers and the tallest glass towers on Earth.
• Indonesia • Asia • Europe • Middle East & Africa • Americas • World's Tallest

✨ PHOTO-LIKE VISUALS
Cinematic camera, skies with clouds, sunsets and starry nights, cities stretching to the horizon, real shadows and glass reflections.

⭐ COLLECT STARS & AN ALBUM
Earn 1–3 stars per level for perfect drops. Every finished tower goes into your Collection Album with its photo and a fun fact about the real building that inspired it.

🎵 MUSIC FOR EVERY REGION
Gamelan-style bells, Asian plucks, European classics, Middle Eastern scales, American jazz and modern synth.

🎯 FAIR CHALLENGE
Speed rises gradually and is the same for every player. Pendulum and rhythmic movement, narrow floors and no-regrow levels for players who love a challenge.

✔ No login, no account — just play
✔ Playable offline
✔ Ads only when you choose to watch one to continue
✔ One-time "Remove ads" option: instant revives with no ads
✔ Free Mode to stack as high as you can

Building names and models are original and only inspired by real landmarks.

Ready to build your tallest tower?
```
